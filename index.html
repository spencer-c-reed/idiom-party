How to play

  1. Split into two teams. On each turn, a reader from the opposing team holds the phone and keeps score.
  2. Synonym Twist: the saying is rebuilt with indirect substitutes. Example: “outer edge of your mouth muscle” means “tip of your tongue.”
  3. Mixed Message: a riddle-like description points to the whole saying. Choose either style or mix them together.
  4. Dinner table: the reader reads the meaning; the team guesses the saying. Pick all 120 cards or one of three categories.
  5. The reader says only what is shown. Only the active team guesses; close wording that clearly identifies the standard phrase counts.
  6. Only the active team may guess. Tap Got it! for a point. You get two skips per turn; revealing an answer uses a skip and earns no point.
  7. When time runs out, check your turn score and missed sayings, then swap roles. Both teams always get the same number of turns; the higher score after reaching the target wins.
